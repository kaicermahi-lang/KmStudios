Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Kasier Mahamud's Showcase app and website handles your information — plainly and transparently.

Last Updated: May 2025
Overview

Kasier Mahamud's Showcase is a personal portfolio application and website designed to display professional video editing work and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.
Data Collection

This app and website does not:

  • Collect any personal information from users
  • Require account registration or login
  • Use cookies for tracking or advertising
  • Run any analytics or user-tracking services
  • Store any data on servers or third-party databases
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, or contacts
External Links & Platforms

This app and website may contain links or redirect buttons to external platforms including:

  • WhatsApp — for project inquiries via direct messaging
  • Email — to send correspondence directly
  • Facebook — to view the public profile page

When you click these links, you are redirected to third-party platforms governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the data practices of those platforms. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

App Permissions

The Android application only requests the minimum permissions required to function:

  • Internet — to load fonts and external link resources

No sensitive permissions (location, camera, storage, contacts, etc.) are requested.

No ads, no tracking SDKs, no analytics libraries are embedded in the app.
Children's Privacy

This app is a professional portfolio and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data whatsoever, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app or website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
