FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your turnaround time?
For YouTube videos (10-15 mins), the standard turnaround is 48 to 72 hours. Short-form content (TikTok/Reels) is typically delivered within 24 hours. Complex cinematic projects take 1-2 weeks depending on the scope.
Do you offer unlimited revisions?
We provide 2 free revisions per project to ensure the video aligns perfectly with your vision. Any massive structural changes after the first draft may incur additional fees.
How do I send you the raw footage?
We recommend using Google Drive, Dropbox, or Frame.io. Simply upload your raw files to a folder and share the link with us. We handle proxies and massive files up to several terabytes.
Do you provide royalty-free music and stock footage?
Yes! We have subscriptions to premium services like Epidemic Sound, Artlist, and Envato Elements. We handle all licensing so you never have to worry about copyright strikes.
